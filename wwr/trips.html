Trip Information

All trips include shuttle, gear, and a gourmet lunch. Prices are per person and all excursions start at 7am

Colorado River Run

Carve the canyons with the powerful Colorado River. The water changes fast and excites with twists and turns and roaring rapids.

Snake River Run

Take in the greenery while on the water at beautiful Snake River. This trip is great for beginners and families with gentle rapids and plenty of fun along the way.

Salt River Run

Experience the beauty of the salt river with a good mix of rapids and relaxation. Take in the views on this breathtaking scenic journey down the salt river.
